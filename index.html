The 90 days in three phases

  • Days 1 to 28: clarify and build trustWho we are, what we source, how to order, what pre-order means, the delivery process, the founder, FAQ videos.
  • Days 29 to 56: build proof and consistencyDelivered packages, reviews, order packing, arrival updates, request-to-delivery case studies, repeat customers.
  • Days 57 to 90: increase interactionPolls, "choose the next pre-order", question stickers, customer request videos, product comparisons, live Q&A, comment replies.

The 90-day calendar

Four posts a week on each platform: We can pick days and agree on that or we can test as and when to know what is working for us. 90 Days. Pick a platform, then click any day. Please not that this is ment for planning purposes, we can adjust anything and also change plans

  • Why this plan and not the daily listThe daily list means 90 posts on three platforms, 270 in total. That is too much and also not realistic based of timeing and outliers. This plan keeps 52 posts per platform, gives each platform its own angle, and builds urgency around three pre-order windows. We kept the guarantee post from the daily list because it answers the question buyers ask most.
